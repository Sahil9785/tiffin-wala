Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our tiffin service in Pilani

Can I pause my weekly or monthly subscription when visiting home?
Yes, absolutely! You can pause your subscription with just 1-click on WhatsApp or via your dashboard at least 2 hours before the delivery slot. Your missed days will be automatically rolled forward.
What are the delivery timings for BKBIET and college hostels?
Lunch is delivered between 12:00 PM and 2:00 PM (sharp at hostel mess/gates), and Dinner is delivered between 7:00 PM and 9:00 PM. Hot and fresh guarantee!
Do you offer pure Jain / No Onion & Garlic meals?
Yes! We prepare separate Jain meals in a dedicated cookware setup with zero onion, garlic, or root vegetables. You can simply select the "Jain" preference tab when ordering.
How does the stainless steel tiffin swap work?
To eliminate single-use plastic waste, your food comes in sanitized 3-tier stainless steel containers. When the delivery rider arrives with your next meal, simply hand back yesterday's washed tiffin box.
